About EDUEXPLORA

EDUEXPLORA is an independent South African online catalogue business focused on practical everyday products for the home, school, study and office environment. We aim to make product discovery and ordering simple by providing clear catalogue information and direct customer support through WhatsApp.

Our catalogue includes home appliances, study and office furniture, stationery and cookware. Product availability is confirmed before payment to ensure customers receive up-to-date stock and delivery information.

Shopping, Delivery & Customer Care

Ordering

Select a product and use the WhatsApp enquiry button. Please provide the product name or SKU, quantity required and delivery area. EDUEXPLORA will confirm availability and the final order amount before payment.

Delivery

Delivery availability and fees depend on the customer's location and the size of the order. The applicable delivery method, estimated dispatch period and delivery charge are communicated and agreed before payment.

Product Confirmation

Because catalogue stock can change, customers receive confirmation of the exact available model, colour and applicable specifications before completing an order.

Returns & Refund Information

Incorrect or Damaged Items

If an item arrives damaged, defective or materially different from the confirmed order, contact EDUEXPLORA as soon as reasonably possible with the order details and supporting photographs so the matter can be assessed.

Return Eligibility

Returns are assessed according to the nature and condition of the product and applicable South African consumer law. Products should be kept complete, with supplied accessories and packaging where reasonably possible.

Refunds / Replacement

Where a return is approved, the appropriate remedy may include replacement, repair or refund depending on the circumstances and applicable consumer rights. Final arrangements are communicated directly to the customer.

This summary is intended as customer information and does not limit any rights customers may have under applicable South African consumer protection law.
